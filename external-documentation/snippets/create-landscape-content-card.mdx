1. Open **Collections** in the Depict portal.
2. Open the collection where you want the card.
3. Click **Content** to add a content card, or open an existing card to edit it.
4. Upload an image or video, or select media from the gallery.
5. Under **Card size**, select **Landscape 3:2**.
6. Add a link, text overlay, or CTA if needed.
7. Click **Add card**, or **Save card** when editing.
8. Position the card in the collection grid.
9. Click **Save** in the collection's save bar to publish the changes.
